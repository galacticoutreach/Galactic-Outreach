# Contributing

Thanks for considering it. This page says what contributing to Galactic
actually means, and how to do it so your report is useful. If you are unsure
about any of it, email hello@galacticoutreach.com and ask.

> **Important**
>
> Galactic is proprietary and the source is not published, so there is nothing
> here to fork and no pull request to open. That is not a brush-off — it means
> the contributions that matter are the ones only you can make. You are using
> the product on your own data, on your own provider, against your own market.
> We are not. What you hit, we cannot find on our own.

## What you can contribute

- **Bug reports.** Something behaved wrongly. This is the most valuable thing
  you can send.
- **Feedback.** Something got in your way, or would be better a different way.
- **Documentation corrections.** Anything on the docs that is wrong, stale or
  confusing.
- **Security vulnerabilities.** These go a different route — see
  [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue for one.

## Where to send it

The **Support screen inside the dashboard** is the best place. It attaches your
email address, your campaign and your browser automatically, which is three
things nobody remembers to include and we always want. It also gives you
something back: the message stays on that screen, showing what you wrote and a
dated trail of what has happened to it since.

If you would rather not sign in, email hello@galacticoutreach.com.

## Before you report

- Check the [changelog](https://galacticoutreach.com/changelog). It may already
  be fixed.
- Try it once more and note exactly what you did. A bug you can reproduce is a
  bug we can fix; one you cannot is a conversation.

## What makes a report useful

Three lines, in this order:

- **What you did** — the steps, in the order you took them.
- **What you expected** — sometimes the bug is that the product is confusing,
  and this is the line that says so.
- **What happened instead** — the actual behaviour, and the exact text of any
  error.

A short description is usually enough. If it involves a specific campaign, a
template or a send, say which — we can look at the row.

Screenshots help for anything visual. Do not paste your lead list, your API
keys or a customer's contact details; we can find the record from your campaign
without them.

## What we do with it

Every report becomes a ticket with a stage. You see it as **Open**, **Fixed**
or **Closed**, with the date it last moved, on the Support screen. The reply
comes by email.

We do not promise a fixed turnaround for bugs and feedback. Security reports
are different and carry a stated commitment — see
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

## What we will not do

- Accept pull requests, since there is no published source to change.
- Take feature work on contract through this repository. If you want something
  built, email us and say so directly.
