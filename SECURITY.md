# Security Policy

## Contact

**hello@galacticoutreach.com**

Galactic sends real email to real people on behalf of its customers, and holds
the contact data behind those campaigns. We treat the security of that as a top
priority. However much effort goes into it, vulnerabilities can still be there.

If you find one, we want to know, so we can fix it quickly. We would like to
ask for your help in protecting our users and our systems.

## Reporting vulnerabilities

- **Do** email your findings to hello@galacticoutreach.com. Put "security" in
  the subject so it is not read as an ordinary support message.
- **Do not** take advantage of the vulnerability or problem you have found.
  That includes reading, changing or deleting data that is not yours — a
  campaign's lead list belongs to the customer who collected it.
- **Do not** reveal the problem to others until it has been resolved.
- **Do not** use attacks on physical security, social engineering, distributed
  denial of service, spam, or applications of third parties. Our sending
  providers, payment processor and database host are out of scope; report
  issues in those to them.
- **Do** send enough to reproduce the problem, so it can be resolved quickly.
  Usually a short description is enough; a complex vulnerability may need more.

## What we promise

- We will respond within **3 business days** with our evaluation of the report
  and an expected resolution date.
- If you have followed the instructions above, we will not take any legal
  action against you in regard to the report.
- We will handle your report in strict confidence, and will not pass your
  personal details to third parties without your permission.
- We will keep you informed of the progress towards resolving the problem.
- In any public information about the problem, we will credit you as the
  discoverer, unless you would rather we did not.
- We aim to resolve every problem as quickly as we can, and would like to play
  an active role in the eventual publication about it once it is fixed.

## Scope

This policy covers **galacticoutreach.com** and the application behind it.

The source is not published, so there is nothing here to read for a
vulnerability — reports come from using the product. That does not make them
less welcome; it makes them harder to find, and more valuable when you do.
